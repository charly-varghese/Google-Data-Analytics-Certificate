## 📌 Course Overview

"Accelerate Your Job Search with AI" is the final career-execution module of the Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate.  
Unlike technical analytics courses, this program focuses on converting skills into career opportunities using AI-powered tools.

This course bridges the gap between certification and employment by teaching structured, ethical, and strategic use of AI in modern job searching.

---

## 🎯 Key Learning Objectives

By completing this course, I learned how to:

- Identify and articulate transferable skills
- Develop a clear career identity statement
- Create a structured AI-assisted job search plan
- Tailor resumes using AI strategically
- Draft personalized cover letters efficiently
- Build a strong professional brand
- Manage job applications with tracking systems
- Prepare for interviews using AI tools
- Practice behavioral responses using the STAR method
- Respond professionally to job offers

## 🧠 AI Tools Practiced

Throughout the course, I applied AI tools in practical career scenarios:

- **Gemini** – Resume tailoring, job search planning, networking strategy, branding
- **NotebookLM** – Interview preparation, document-based Q&A, audio summaries
- **Gemini Deep Research** – Company and role research for interview preparation
- AI-assisted tracking in Google Sheets

The focus was on ethical and strategic AI use — not dependency.
